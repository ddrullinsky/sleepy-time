🌙 Bedtime Stars

A bedtime-routine checklist for kids, with a star-based prize tracker and optional two-phone sync through iCloud.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sync between two phones?

On the first phone, tap the gear icon → Generate a Random Code (or type your own). On the second phone, enter the exact same code. Checks made on either phone appear on the other within seconds. Both phones must be signed into iCloud.

The checklist was full last night — why is it empty this morning?

That's by design: the checklist resets every day so tonight's routine starts fresh. Stars from completed nights are kept.

We missed a night — where did our stars go?

A missed night resets the star tracker the next morning. A prize that was already earned (10 stars) is never taken away.

Sync isn't working.

  1. Check both phones are signed into iCloud (Settings app → your name).
  2. Check both phones have the exact same family code (gear icon → Settings).
  3. Tap Test Connection in the app's Settings for a diagnosis.
